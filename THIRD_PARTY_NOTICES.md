## Original approval stamp

`assets/exemples/approved.svg` is original report-rs artwork under the project
MIT license. Its letters are constructed from geometric paths, without external
fonts or third-party artwork. `assets/exemples/approved.png` is rendered from
that SVG, and the same PNG is stored in `examples/test.sqlite3`, in
`restaurants.approved` for row `id = 2`.

These replace the PNGEgg approval stamp (marked non-commercial use) on
2026-09-03. The original PNGEgg image is not covered by the MIT license.
To regenerate the PNG: `rsvg-convert assets/exemples/approved.svg -o assets/exemples/approved.png`.
